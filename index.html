Social Media Foundation

First, we get the story straight.

Before the cameras roll and the posts go live, we need to know what we're actually talking about.

That's where your Social Media Foundation comes in.

We get to know your business, figure out what your socials need to say and build a creative direction worth following.

A little thinking upfront. A lot less guesswork later.

Where are we starting from?

Already doing your thing?

You've got a brand. You've got socials. Maybe things just need a little shaking up.

We'll look at what's working, what's not and where your communication can get better.

Your Foundation focuses on: Account review, communication voice, storytelling pillars, visual direction and a profile refresh.

Starting from scratch?

New business? First Instagram page? Welcome to the internet.

We'll help establish your social presence, find your voice and figure out the stories worth telling.

Your Foundation focuses on: Communication voice, storytelling pillars, visual references, profile setup and initial launch direction.

Still figuring things out?

Not quite sure what your brand stands for yet?

That might take a little more than a content calendar.

We'll help identify what needs sorting first and whether a bigger brand strategy conversation is needed.

Your Foundation focuses on: What needs sorting first, and whether a brand strategy conversation should come before the posting.

What goes into your Foundation?

01 Getting to know you.

Your business, your audience, your goals and the things that make you interesting.

02 Finding your voice.

How your business should show up, sound and communicate on social media.

03 The stories worth telling.

We develop focused content pillars around what people should know, understand and remember about your business.

04 Getting the look right.

Visual mood boards, creative references and content formats that give your socials a consistent direction.

05 Setting things up.

We review or establish your social media profile essentials, making it easier for people to understand your business and get in touch.

06 The first chapter.

We work out the initial editorial priorities so we're ready to move from ideas to actual content.

Before the posting comes the thinking.

Your Foundation gives us a direction to work from, rather than figuring things out one random post at a time.

It's about how your business communicates on social media. If you need to build or rethink your entire brand, that's a separate conversation.

Can you handle our social media from scratch?

Yep. Whether you're starting fresh or improving an existing page, we'll establish the social media direction before we start creating content.

It can help people discover your business, understand what you offer and get curious enough to take the next step.
