Let's Talk

Good to know.

Do you manage social media?

Yep. From ideas and content creation to publishing and reporting, we can take care of the whole thing.

Can I hire Pocket for just one video?

Absolutely. One video, a content shoot or something a little more involved. Tell us what you have in mind.

Do you work on monthly retainers?

Yes. Our ongoing social media partnerships begin with a three-month commitment. Standalone content projects are quoted separately.

Can you work with our existing marketing team?

Of course. If you've already got someone managing your socials, we can step in to create the content.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you're looking to create or manage. Share your brief, and we'll recommend an appropriate scope and quotation.

Do you guarantee bookings or sales?

We can't, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Social media can absolutely bring in enquiries and sales, but the end result also depends on your product, pricing, customer experience and paid campaigns. Our job is making sure your brand is worth noticing.

What about revisions?

Our standard scopes include two consolidated revision rounds. Any additional requirements are discussed before proceeding.
